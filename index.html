FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Chhaya do?+
Chhaya is a data analytics platform that helps businesses track performance, understand trends and create clear reports.
Can I connect my existing data sources?+
Yes. Chhaya can be designed to connect sales, marketing, website and customer data sources in one place.
Do you offer a free consultation?+
Yes. We offer an initial consultation to understand your business, data, reporting requirements, and the problems you want to solve.
What type of businesses do you work with?+
We work with businesses across different industries and build analytics solutions based on their specific business needs, goals, and data.
Do I need to have clean data already?+
No. We can help with data cleaning, transformation, structuring, and preparation before building the final analytics solution.
What tools do you use?+
Our primary tools include Power BI, Excel, SQL, Python, and Power Query, depending on the project requirements.
Can you build a dashboard according to my business requirements?+
Yes. Every dashboard can be designed around your KPIs, business questions, workflow, and reporting requirements rather than using a one-size-fits-all template.
Can you automate my regular reports?+
Yes. Where technically possible, we can help automate data preparation, reporting workflows, and dashboard refresh processes to reduce repetitive manual work.
